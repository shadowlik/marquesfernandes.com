I recently started a project using the [Vuetify](http://vuetifyjs.com) , framework [see](https://vuejs.org) of components Material, and however complete and well structured the components are, I had some difficulty in composing and combining the interface. If you, like me, have no design tact, check out this list of sites and projects to help inspire:

## [Made with Material](https://madewithmaterial.tumblr.com)

[![Made with Material](./2019-09-image-2.jpg)](https://madewithmaterial.tumblr.com/)

[Made with Material](https://madewithmaterial.tumblr.com/) it is a [Tumblr](https://www.tumblr.com/) which brings together a beautiful portfolio of apps created with Material.

## [Dribble: Material Design](https://dribbble.com/tags/material_design)

[![Dribble: Material Design](./2019-09-image-3.jpg)](https://dribbble.com/tags/material_design)

[Dribble](https://dribbble.com/) is an online community for the exhibition of artistic content.  You can filter by tags of interest, for example: [tags/material\_design](https://dribbble.com/tags/material_design) .

## [Behance: Material Design Projects](https://www.behance.net/search?content=projects&search=material%20design&sort=appreciations&time=week&user_tags=89571585)

[![Behance: Material Design Projects](./2019-09-image-4.jpg)](https://www.behance.net/search?content=projects&search=material%20design&sort=appreciations&time=week&user_tags=89571585)

[behance](https://www.behance.net/) (Bēhance) is a social network of [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/br/) which aims to "show and discover creative work". In it you will find a series of inspirations including samples made with [Material Design](https://www.behance.net/search?content=projects&search=material%20design&sort=appreciations&time=week&user_tags=89571585) .

## [Material Patterns](https://materialpatterns.tumblr.com)

[![Material Patterns](./2019-09-image-5.jpg)](https://materialpatterns.tumblr.com)

[Material Patterns](https://materialpatterns.tumblr.com/) it is a [Tumblr](https://www.tumblr.com/) which brings together some examples of apps created with Material.

## [Material Kit by Creative Tim](https://demos.creative-tim.com/material-kit/index.html)

[![Material Kit by Creative Tim](./2019-09-image-6.jpg)](https://demos.creative-tim.com/material-kit/index.html)

[Kit material](https://demos.creative-tim.com/material-kit/index.html) is a free UI kit built on top of Bootstrap 4 with a design inspired by Google Material.

## [Android Uplabs](https://www.uplabs.com/android)

[![Android Uplabs](./2019-09-image-8.jpg)](https://www.uplabs.com/android)

"THE [UpLabs](https://www.uplabs.com/android) it's the place to find high-quality design resources for designers, creative agencies and developers."

## [Material Design Blog](http://materialdesignblog.com/material-design-showcase/)

[![Material Design Blog](./2019-09-image-7.jpg)](http://materialdesignblog.com/material-design-showcase/)

"A portfolio of the best material design websites, themes, models and examples of concepts for your inspiration. Creative projects built with Material Design."

## [Pinterest: Material Design](https://br.pinterest.com/search/pins/?rs=ac&len=2&q=material%20design&eq=material%20design&etslf=6060&term_meta[]=material%7Cautocomplete%7C2&term_meta[]=design%7Cautocomplete%7C2)

[![Pinterest: Material Design](./2019-09-image-10.jpg)](https://br.pinterest.com/search/pins/?rs=ac&len=2&q=material%20design&eq=material%20design&etslf=6060&term_meta[]=material%7Cautocomplete%7C2&term_meta[]=design%7Cautocomplete%7C2)

[pintrest](https://dribbble.com/) is an online community for the exhibition of artistic content.  You can filter by tags of interest, for example: [tags/material\_design](https://br.pinterest.com/search/pins/?rs=ac&len=2&q=material%20design&eq=material%20design&etslf=6060&term_meta[]=material%7Cautocomplete%7C2&term_meta[]=design%7Cautocomplete%7C2)

## [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/fresh-ui-inspiration-in-the-era-of-google-material-and-design-patterns.html)

[![Awwwards](./2019-09-image-11.jpg)](https://www.awwwards.com/fresh-ui-inspiration-in-the-era-of-google-material-and-design-patterns.html)

[Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/) (Awwwards Online SL) is a website that promotes web design and web development competitions. The aim is to recognize and promote the best of innovative web design. [in January 2019](https://www.awwwards.com/fresh-ui-inspiration-in-the-era-of-google-material-and-design-patterns.html) they ran an article promoting Material Design interface inspirations.

## [Codepen: Material Design](https://codepen.io/search/pens?q=Material%20Design)

[![Codepen: Material Design](./2019-09-image-9.png)](https://codepen.io/search/pens?q=Material%20Design)

" [codepen](https://codepen.io/search/pens?q=Material%20Design) is a prototyping service that allows you to share code." Filtering by [tag](https://codepen.io/search/pens?q=Material%20Design) you can also find some examples of snippets made with Material.
