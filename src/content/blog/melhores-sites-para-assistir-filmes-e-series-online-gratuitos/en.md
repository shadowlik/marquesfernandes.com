There are several platforms to watch movies online for free, without even needing to download any program. If you are looking for sites with up-to-date and even alternative movies, know that there is certainly some platform that will interest you. I gathered some popular platforms to watch movies, series and even documentaries online.

Finding sites to watch series and movies in HD may not be an easy task; there are several sites and many of them turn out not to be safe, asking you to download and install dubious extensions on your computer. That's why I put together a list of the best sites for you to watch movies and series online in HD for free.

## [AZMovies](https://azm.to/)

![AZMovies](./2020-08-screenshot-azm.to-2020.08.10-17_11_51.jpg)

AZMovies is another popular and long-standing streaming site on this list. An excellent collection of movies is available in great streaming qualities, including 1080p and 720p.

The site's slogan says "Watch your favorite movies here without limits, just choose the movie you like and enjoy! It is free and always will be." Check out AZMovies today!

## [Popcornflix](https://www.popcornflix.com/pages/discover/d/movies)

![Popcornflix](./2020-08-screenshot-www.popcornflix.com-2020.08.10-17_20_17.jpg)

Popcornflix is another great place to watch movies online for free. Constantly updated with new movies from Screen Media Ventures.

Popcornflix has more than 1500 movies that include comedy, drama, horror, action, romance, family, documentaries and foreign films. They also feature web originals and film school originals.

No account is required on Popcornflix; just click **Play** on the chosen movie and enjoy.

## [Vudu](https://www.vudu.com/content/movies/uxrow/Movies/5777)

![Vudu](./2020-08-screenshot-www.vudu_.com-2020.08.10-17_28_30.jpg)

Vudu may not be your first choice when looking for free movie streaming sites, but there are *thousands* of movies here that you can watch right now. All you have to do is put up with a few commercials. Some of this site's newest additions include *Slow Burn, Facing Ali, King Arthur, Frailty, Gordy* and *Tamara.*

One excellent thing about Vudu's movies is that some of them are in 1080p, so you don't have to sacrifice quality just to watch a few free movies.

## [Tubi](https://tubitv.com/)

![Tubi](./2020-08-screenshot-tubitv.com-2020.08.10-17_26_06.jpg)

Tubi has become a very popular site for streaming free movies and TV shows, without the need for a subscription. Tubi provides thousands of free movies and TV series, but, like many others on this list, it is ad-supported.

Tubi is also available as an app on the Amazon App Store, Roku Channel list, Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Check out our tutorial for more information about Tubi.

Tubi has thousands of free movies and TV shows that you can stream right now. Some of them can only be rented and not watched for free, but many of them *are completely free* to stream.

There are dozens of genres you can choose from on Tubi, including the regular ones for romance, drama, documentary, kids, comedy and horror movies, as well as exclusive genres like *Home and Garden*, *Preschool* and *Sword and Sorcery.*

## [Stremio Web (Alpha)](https://app.strem.io/#/)

![Stremio Web (Alpha)](./2020-08-screenshot-app.strem_.io-2020.08.10-17_32_59.jpg)

[Stremio](https://app.strem.io/#/) is a very well-known platform for watching videos via [to](http://marquesfernandes.com/tecnologia/como-faco-para-baixar-um-torrent/)[rrent](http://marquesfernandes.com/tecnologia/como-faco-para-baixar-um-torrent/). It has an extremely complete library; if the movie exists in torrent format you can watch it there. They are currently working on an alpha browser version; it has several bugs while watching, but it is definitely worth checking out.

## [SuperFlix](https://www.superflix.net/)

[![SuperFlix](./2020-11-image-7.jpg)](https://www.superflix.net/)

[SuperFlix](https://www.superflix.net/) reached me through the comments; I didn't know the platform and was surprised by the availability of movies. The amount of ads is something that bothers quite a bit; every now and then, clicks around the site open a popup to another advertising site, but nothing new on sites of this kind. It has plenty of subtitled and dubbed options of movies and series. With a pleasant interface and its broad catalog, it is a good option for those looking to watch series and movies online for free.

## [MegaFilmes](https://megafilmes.org/)

![Mega Filmes](./2021-01-image.jpg)

[MegaFilmes](https://megafilmes.org/) is a simple site, entirely in Portuguese and functional. All the content is available both subtitled and dubbed. But get ready: the amount of ads is annoying; you may need to make 5 clicks just to be able to access a link. In addition to movies, the site also has television programs and series.
