Podcasts have been gaining a lot of popularity lately and taking over the daily routine of Brazilians. Podcasts are an evolution of radio, aligning technology and practicality, they have become the perfect medium to stay tuned or even to relax in the most diverse subjects. While on the go, exercising or even working, an ocean of content just a headset away, podcasting is a great way to optimize your time and stay informed.

I have some difficulty finding new content, even on the [spotify](https://open.spotify.com/user/12142144766?si=3pJxPV5oTj-Ox5eueBglDw) , and that's why I've separated a list of all the channels I usually listen to, from the most technical ones focused on development and innovation, to personal development content and the corporate world. If you like to keep up with the news on the market, don't miss this list!

*I will keep this list up to date and gradually post my opinion on all the channels listed. Remembering that I'm always looking for new content so please leave your suggestion in the comments* s!
