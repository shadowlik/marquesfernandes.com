Looking for an internship is not an easy task; with the rush of college, jobs and personal life, it becomes tiring work. But today the market has evolved and the internet has become the main way to find job openings, with big companies creating solutions to make finding your dream internship easier.

With so many free tools available to find an internship, which one should you choose? To try to help, I put together a list of the best websites to look for an internship online.

## [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/)

[![LinkedIn](./2020-11-image-9.jpg)](https://www.linkedin.com/)

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) is probably the largest platform for those looking for an internship. Initially created with the goal of being a professional social network, today it is much more than that; it consolidates your résumé and gives you access to many internship opportunities on the market. It is one of the most popular options for those who want to keep a visible professional profile and expand their network of contacts.

## [ABRE](https://portalabre.com.br)

[![ABRE](./2020-11-image-20.jpg)](https://portalabre.com.br)

[ABRE](https://portalabre.com.br) (Brazilian Employment and Internship Agency) is one of the largest internship companies in Brazil. Operating since 2004, it has been transforming the market across the country. It operates nationwide and the entire selection process is done online; you can register your résumé and wait for contact from one of the company's intermediaries, or actively search for the openings offered on the portal.

## [CIEE](https://portal.ciee.org.br/)

[![CIEE](./2020-11-image-19-1.jpg)](https://portal.ciee.org.br/)

The Center for Company-School Integration (CIEE) is a private, non-profit, non-economic civil association, recognized as a social assistance entity that, through various programs, including apprenticeship and student internships, provides teenagers and young people with comprehensive training, bringing them into the world of work.

## [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com.br)

[![Glassdoor](./2020-11-image-10-1.jpg)](https://www.glassdoor.com.br)

[Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com.br) started as a portal to professionally review companies, having recently acquired one of its competitors, Love Mondays. The site allows anonymous reviews of companies by employees, providing a trustworthy environment to rate and rank the listed companies on various points. The portal now also allows you to register a profile with your professional résumé and search for internship openings.

## [Walljobs](https://www.walljobs.com.br/)

[![Walljobs](./2020-11-image-20-1.jpg)](https://www.walljobs.com.br/)

[Walljobs](https://www.walljobs.com.br/) doesn't only show internship openings, but it also works for those who are looking for this kind of position. You can view the opportunities without having to register, but to contact an employer you need to sign up on the site.

## [Nube](https://www.nube.com.br/)

[![Nube](./2020-11-image-21-1.jpg)](https://www.nube.com.br/)

[Nube](https://www.nube.com.br/) is one of the most used sites by interns in Brazil. It is government-backed, and besides letting you register your résumé and search for openings, it also offers tips on how to handle an interview, improve your résumé, and even emails you about available openings. Today it is one of the largest private corporations for placing young people into the job market.
