[VS Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/) is my darling editor at the time: Open source, cross-platform, lightweight, highly customizable and with an amazing community. If you haven't tried it yet, try it!

***Also check:*** [*Icon Packs for VS Code*](http://marquesfernandes.com/2019/10/07/pacotes-de-icones-para-vs-code-2019)

I separated a list with 6 themes that I have already used and approved:

## [0\. night owl](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=sdras.night-owl)

One of the darlings of the community, the Nigh Owl theme was developed by [Sarah Drasner](https://github.com/sdras) and it has optimized colors for those who like to work in the dark.

[![night owl](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sdras/night-owl-vscode-theme/master/preview.png)](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=sdras.night-owl)

## [1\. Dracula](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=dracula-theme.theme-dracula)

Another darling of the community and currently my topic of choice. A dark theme with strong yet pleasant combinations and contrasts. It has packages for almost all IDEs on the market and is also available for terminals, messaging applications and much more.

![Image result for dracula official vscode](https://camo.githubusercontent.com/ce197121bfb22f8c447fde974b7ed374a6bb92b0/68747470733a2f2f647a776f6e73656d72697368372e636c6f756466726f6e742e6e65742f6974656d732f327133723135343332733348303030313375316a2f53637265656e25323053686f74253230323031382d30362d3032253230617425323031302e31362e30312e706e67)

## [two. One Dark Pro](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=zhuangtongfa.Material-theme)

For those who are migrating from [atom,](https://ide.atom.io/) One Dark Pro will make you feel at home.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/0_vmc8bpndn19OttSk.png)

## [4\. Noctis](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=liviuschera.noctis)

With soft combinations this is a theme for those looking to rest their eyes. It has light and dark variations.

## [5\. One Monokai Theme](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=azemoh.one-monokai)

[Joshua Azemoh](https://github.com/azemoh) decided to cross his One Dark Pro theme with the theme [monokai](https://www.monokai.pro/) , bringing the One Monokai Theme to life.

[![One Monokai Theme](https://github.com/azemoh/vscode-one-monokai/raw/master/screenshot-v0.2.0.png)](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=azemoh.one-monokai)

## [6\. Monokai Pro](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=monokai.theme-monokai-pro-vscode)

[Monokai Pro](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=monokai.theme-monokai-pro-vscode) is a color scheme, custom interface and also a very complete collection of icons for Visual Studio Code.

![Monokai Pro](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Monokai/monokai-pro-vscode/master/img/monokai-pro.png)

## [7\. Shades of Purple](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=ahmadawais.shades-of-purple)

A professional theme with several handpicked vibrant shades of purple for your VS Code editor and terminal.

[![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahmadawais/shades-of-purple-vscode/master/images/1_sop.gif)](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=ahmadawais.shades-of-purple)
