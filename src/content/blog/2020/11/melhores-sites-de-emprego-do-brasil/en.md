Looking for a job is not an easy task, but the days when job hunting meant pounding the pavement or searching newspaper classifieds are gone. The market has evolved and today the internet has become the main way to find job openings.

Today we have large companies that let you register your resume, post job openings and even run a more customized process for your career transition.

With so many tools available, finding the job of your dreams can become an easy task. I've put together a list of the best sites to look for a job online, both free and paid.

## [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/)

[![LinkedIn](./2020-11-image-9.jpg)](https://www.linkedin.com/)

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) is probably the largest platform for those looking for a job. Initially created with the intent of being a professional social network, today it is much more than that, consolidating your resume and giving you access to many job opportunities on the market. It is one of the most popular options for those who want to keep a visible professional profile and expand their network of contacts. It offers a subscription service that broadens contact possibilities and gives your resume greater visibility to recruiters.

## [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com.br)

[![Glassdoor](./2020-11-image-10-1.jpg)](https://www.glassdoor.com.br)

[Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com.br) started as a portal for professionally reviewing companies, and recently acquired one of its competitors, Love Mondays. The site allows anonymous employee reviews of companies, providing a trustworthy environment to rate and rank the listed companies on various points. The portal now also allows you to register a profile with your professional resume and search for job openings.

## [Vagas.com](https://www.vagas.com.br/)

[![Vagas.com](./2020-11-image-10-3.jpg)](https://www.vagas.com.br/)

[Vagas](http://www.vagas.com.br/) is also a free job site where companies from all over the country can offer their selection processes without the candidate having to pay anything for it. Most listings provide their respective tests through the site itself, which makes the process easier for both the candidate and the recruiter.

According to the site, there are currently more than 3,000 registered companies and many openings are offered daily.

Registration is free, and the candidate won't need to pay to sign up or use the portal's resources, such as the support materials.

## [Revelo](https://www.revelo.com.br/)

[![Revelo](./2020-11-image-6-9.jpg)](https://www.revelo.com.br/)

[Revelo](https://www.revelo.com.br/) is a company born with a lot of technology and also focused on that market. Created in 2014 with the goal of solving the problem companies face in finding talent quickly and efficiently, it claims to deliver selection processes 5 times faster. As the largest technology company in the human resources market in Latin America, it relies on a 100% online and efficient system.  

## [Trampos](https://trampos.co/)

[![Trampos](./2020-11-image-6-7.jpg)](https://trampos.co/)

[Trampos](http://trampos.co/) originated on Twitter, where it shared the selection processes of communication and marketing companies. As its popularity grew, it was able to develop its own platform, and today it is a go-to job site for those looking for a position in those areas.

Its search system is also free, but it offers subscribers the advantage of accessing openings 24 hours in advance.

## [Infojobs](https://www.infojobs.com.br/)

[![Infojobs](./2020-11-image-18.png)](https://www.infojobs.com.br/)

[InfoJobs](http://www.infojobs.com.br/) has both a free and a paid business model, giving advantages to paying users in their job search. With 250,000 openings and more than 220,000 registered companies, it is one of the largest portals for those looking for a job. It provides various statistics per opening, such as average candidate age, education level, language fluency and many other characteristics of those competing in the same selection process.

## [Catho](https://www.catho.com.br/)

[![Catho](./2020-11-image-15.png)](https://www.catho.com.br/)

[Catho](https://www.catho.com.br/) is one of the most popular job sites, although it has been losing ground to its competitors, both for many people looking for a job and for those looking for professionals. The platform was one of the pioneers in this field and works like a classifieds board where various companies can post their job openings.

One downside is that Catho has a charging system on the side of those looking for a job, and not the employer like its competitors, but during the first 30 days, access to the site is free so that people can test out the features.

## [Trabalha Brasil](https://www.trabalhabrasil.com.br/)

[![Trabalha Brasil](./2020-11-image-7.png)](https://www.trabalhabrasil.com.br/)

The site [Trabalha Brasil](https://www.trabalhabrasil.com.br/) (formerly SINE - Site Nacional de Empregos) is an online nationwide job classifieds platform, and completely free. Trabalha Brasil is an online job classifieds board that promotes direct contact between those looking for a job and those who are hiring.  
Developed by Ponte Azul Inovação e Tecnologia, the site relies on HR and Technology specialists who ensure a trustworthy and secure environment.  
And on top of all that, it's free, because our biggest goal is to contribute to a Brazil where everyone works, without complications.
