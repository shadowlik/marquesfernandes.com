## 1\. [Snow](https://themeisle.com/themes/neve/)

[![Snow Theme](/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/image-17-1024x502.jpg)](https://themeisle.com/themes/neve/)

Snow is a super fast, easily customizable and multipurpose theme. It's perfect for blogs, small businesses, startups, agencies, businesses, e-commerce stores (WooCommerce store), as well as personal portfolio sites and most types of projects. A fully optimized and responsive theme for the AMP, Neve loads in a few seconds and adapts perfectly to any viewing device. Although lightweight and minimalist in design, the theme is highly extensible but has SEO-optimized code, resulting in better rankings in Google search results. Neve works perfectly with Gutenberg and the most popular page builders (Elementor, Brizy, Beaver Builder, Visual Composer, SiteOrigin, Divi). Neve is also WooCommerce ready, responsive, RTL and translation ready. Look no further. Snow is the perfect theme for you!

## two. [Zakra](https://zakratheme.com)

[![Zakra Theme](/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/image-18-1024x507.jpg)](https://zakratheme.com)

Zakra is a flexible, fast, light and modern multi-purpose theme that comes with many free sites for beginners (currently more than 10 free sites are added later) that you can use to make your site beautiful and professional. Check all startup sites at https://zakratheme.com/demos. Suitable for personal blog, portfolio, WooCommerce stores, business websites and niche websites (such as cafe, spa, charity, yoga, wedding, dentist, education etc.) as well. It works with Elementor and other main page makers, so you can create any layout you like. Theme is responsive, Gutenberg compatible, SEO compliant, translation ready, and compatible with all major WordPress plugins.

## 3\. [Astra](https://justfreethemes.com/astra/)

[![Astra Theme](/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/image-19-1024x507.jpg)](https://justfreethemes.com/astra/)

Astra is a fast, fully customizable and beautiful WordPress theme suitable for blog, personal portfolio, business website and WooCommerce store. It's very lightweight (less than 50KB on the front end) and offers unrivaled speed. Built with SEO in mind, Astra comes with Schema.org code built in and is native AMP ready for search engines to love your site. It offers special features and templates to work seamlessly with all page makers like Elementor, Beaver Builder, Visual Composer, SiteOrigin, Divi, etc. Some of the other features:

-   Ready for WooCommerce
-   Responsive
-   RTL and ready for translation
-   Extendable with premium add-ons
-   Updated regularly
-   Designed, Developed, Maintained and Supported by Brainstorm Force.

## 4\. [customize](https://pressmaximum.com/customify/)

[![Customize theme](/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/image-20-1024x507.jpg)](https://pressmaximum.com/customify/)

Customify is a fast, lightweight, responsive and super flexible multipurpose theme, built with SEO, speed and usability in mind. Unleash the power of your imagination with a true WYSIWYG Header & Footer builder (within WordPress Customizer) created exclusively for this theme. Theme works great with any of your favorite page makers like Elementor, Beaver Builder, SiteOrigin, Thrive Architect, Divi, Visual Composer, etc. Combined with Header & Footer builder, you can create any type of website, such as stores, commercial agencies, corporate, portfolio, education, university portal, consulting, church, restaurant, medicine and so on. Customify supports all well-coded plugins, including major ones like WooCommerce, OrbitFox, Yoast, BuddyPress, bbPress, etc.

## 7\. [GeneratePress](https://generatepress.com/)

[![GeneratePress Theme](/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/image-23-1024x507.jpg)](https://generatepress.com/)

GeneratePress is a lightweight WordPress theme, built with a focus on speed and usability. Performance matters to us, which is why a fresh install of GeneratePress adds less than 15kb (gzipped) to your page size. We take full advantage of the new block editor (Gutenberg), which gives you more control over the creation of your content. If you use page builders, GeneratePress is the right theme for you. It is fully compatible with all major page makers including Beaver Builder and Elementor. Thanks to our emphasis on WordPress coding standards, we can have full compatibility with all well-coded plugins, including WooCommerce. GeneratePress is fully responsive, uses valid HTML / CSS, and is translated into over 25 languages by our amazing user community. Some of our many features include microdata integration, 9 widget areas, 5 navigation locations, 5 sidebar layouts, drop-down menus (click or hover) and navigation color presets.

Its unique feature includes widgetized drag / drop / reorder area, advanced custom widgets, advanced layout options, breaking news options, featured image options for blog / category / archive pages and single page / post integration social media, ad ready, breadcrumb trail, WooCommerce and Page Builder Ready, and more. Create your website without touching the code.
